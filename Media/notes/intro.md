# Notes

Every concept I meet gets its own note, linked to the ideas it builds on and to the project code that uses it. When something breaks, I search for the symptom and land on the explanation, the code, and the last time I hit it. Some notes are drafted with AI help and checked by me; the write-ups below are bugs I hit myself.
